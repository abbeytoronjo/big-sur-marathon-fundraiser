01 — WHY I'M RUNNING

Running has been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

Running has been part of my life from a young age through college, where I competed as an NCAA Division I cross country and track athlete. Running has introduced me to countless people, experiences, and memories that mean so much to me, and it’s a sport that has continued to be an important part of my life long after college.

I love that Girls on the Run gives young girls a chance to find their own place in the sport. Running is so much more than just running. It’s a source of confidence, friendship, and community. That’s what makes supporting Girls on the Run Central Coast especially meaningful to me.
