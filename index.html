Why I'm running

This is about running. This is not about running.

Running has been part of my life from a young age through college, where I competed as an NCAA Division I cross country and track athlete. Running has introduced me to countless people, experiences, and memories that mean so much to me, and it’s a sport that has continued to be an important part of my life long after college.

I love that Girls on the Run is dedicated to empowering young girls through the joy of running. Running was never meant to be just about running.

This marathon is an opportunity to give back to a sport that has given me so much while supporting an organization that helps make those same experiences possible for the next generation of young runners.

The cause

Girls on the Run Central Coast

Helping girls discover the joy of running.

Learn more about Girls on the Run Central Coast

Girls on the Run uses running and physical activity to help girls build confidence, develop life skills, and create a strong sense of community.

Registration is $280 per girl. It covers ninety-minute sessions with certified volunteer coaches, curriculum and materials, a journal, a team T-shirt, and entry and a medal for the season-ending 5K. Financial aid is available on a sliding scale, and donations help make that possible.
