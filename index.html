01 — WHY I'M RUNNING

Running has been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

I grew up running and continued through college as an NCAA Division I cross country and track athlete. Running has given me community, confidence, and some of my favorite memories.

I never had the opportunity to participate in Girls on the Run, but I love its mission of empowering young girls through running. This marathon is an opportunity to give back to a sport that has given me so much.

02 — THE CAUSE

GIRLS ON THE RUN CENTRAL COAST

Helping girls discover the joy of running.

Girls on the Run uses running and physical activity to help girls build confidence, develop life skills, and create a strong sense of community.
