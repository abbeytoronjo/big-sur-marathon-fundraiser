01 — WHY I'M RUNNING

Running has been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

Running has been part of my life from a young age, all the way through college as an NCAA Division I cross country and track athlete. It has given me community, confidence, and some of my favorite memories.

I never had the opportunity to participate in Girls on the Run, but I love its mission and dedication to empowering young girls through the joy of running. This marathon is an opportunity to give back to a sport that has given me so much.
